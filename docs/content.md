Chapter 1 – Introduction

Background of the study system
Renewable energy and sustainable technologies have become very important in addressing global environmental challenges such as climate change, greenhouse gas emissions and running out of fossil fuels. In the past, energy production mainly depended on non-renewable sources such as coal, oil and natural gas which will contributed substantially to environmental pollution. As technology advances and environmental awareness increases, renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, hydroelectric energy have now gained more attention worldwide.

In current trends, the development of electric vehicles, smart cities have accelerated due to the leveraging of Internet of Things sensors, Artificial Intelligence, and data analytics to optimize municipal services [1]. Governments, organizations and communities are using more green technology to create less pollution and protect the environment. However still have many people lack of awareness and understanding of these technologies and their benefits.

Therefore, Green Technology Club was created to share information about renewable energy, electric vehicles, smart cities and sustainable technologies. Through this website, user can participate in green technology activities and increase their awareness.

Target audience
The target audience of Green Technology Club website can be viewed from many perspectives such as students, teenagers and those who interested in learning about green technology and sustainable technologies. Through this website, university student can gain some knowledge about renewable energy and participate in the environmental activity. Not only that, organizations
may also use this website as a resource to promote awareness.






Problem description
Although Green technology information is already available on the internet but it is still scattered among various websites and platforms making it hard for user to find reliable and organized information. Furthermore, many university environmental clubs still use traditional communication methods such as social media and email that may not be the best approach to handle activities, announcements and member engagement.

Without a dedication website or platform, club members may face some difficulties to access information about upcoming events, learning resources and green technology updates. Hence, a centralized website is needed to better manage information and improve communication between club members and users.

Significance
The Green Technology Club website provides many benefits to users by creating an organized platform to promote sustainability awareness. This system allows users easily access the information about renewable energy, electric vehicles, smart cities and sustainable technologies.

For the club members, this website improves their communication, event management and encourages more participation in green activities. Besides that, for general users it provides educational resources that can improve their knowledge and understanding on environmental issues so that it can inspire them to lead more sustainable lifestyles.

Overall, this system promotes the awareness of green technology and helps to build a more environmentally conscious community.
