## **Green Tech Club Website — Project Overview**

### **Project Background**

The **Green Tech Club Website** is an immersive, story-driven website designed for a university Green Technology Club. Rather than functioning as a traditional club website, it presents sustainability as a journey—from understanding the environmental challenges caused by fossil fuels to discovering renewable energy, innovative green technologies, student-led projects, and ultimately inspiring visitors to become part of the solution.

The website combines **cinematic storytelling**, **interactive animations**, **modern web technologies**, and **educational content** to create an engaging experience suitable for students, educators, visitors, and potential club members.

The overall narrative follows:

> **Past → Present → Future**
> **Problem → Innovation → Action**

Users begin by confronting the environmental cost of traditional energy, then progressively explore renewable energy, cutting-edge green technologies, the club's real-world initiatives, and finally envision a sustainable future before learning more about the organization and how to get involved.

# Website Structure

## 1. Landing Page — *The Beginning*

**Purpose**
Create an emotional first impression and introduce the central question of the website.

**Theme**
Traditional fossil fuels powered human civilization, but they also created today's environmental challenges.

**Experience**

* Cinematic scroll-driven storytelling
* Industrial cityscape
* Carbon emissions
* Factory silhouettes
* Particle effects
* GSAP ScrollTrigger animations
* Ends with a call-to-action:

> **Can You Help Us Save the World?**

Users then enter the main website.

# Design Philosophy

The website is designed as an interactive narrative rather than a collection of independent pages.

Instead of simply presenting information, each page advances the story:

```text
The Cost of the Past
        ↓
Understanding Energy
        ↓
Exploring Green Innovation
        ↓
Real Student Projects
        ↓
Interactive Learning
        ↓
A Vision of the Future
        ↓
Become Part of the Change
```

The visual language combines **minimalism**, **cinematic storytelling**, **3D-inspired motion**, **glassmorphism**, and **scroll-driven animations** to deliver an experience that feels closer to an interactive documentary than a conventional university club website. The ultimate goal is not only to inform visitors about green technology but also to inspire curiosity, participation, and action.
